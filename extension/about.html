Dictionary

This extension uses the JMdict dictionary files. These files are the property of the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group, and are used in conformance with the Group's licence (CC BY-SA 4.0). YomiScan selects the English glosses and builds lookup indexes from them; the dictionary is not covered by YomiScan's MIT license.

Word boundaries and readings come from UniDic (© The UniDic Consortium, BSD-3-Clause), built into the lindera tokenizer.

Downloaded on first use

Not part of the extension: downloaded from Hugging Face after you agree, checked against pinned SHA-256 hashes, and kept in Chrome's storage for this extension.
