Welcome to YomiScan

Read manga in Japanese, and study any bubble: its words, readings, meanings, grammar and an English translation. Everything runs on your computer.

Start reading

  1. Pin YomiScan: click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next to YomiScan.
  2. Open a manga chapter whose pages are images.
  3. Click the YomiScan icon. A panel opens on the page; choose how to read.

Read Chapter

Keeps the Japanese. Each bubble gets an outline: click one, or press J / K to go to the next or previous bubble, and its study card opens.

Select Text

Drag a box around any Japanese text on any page. Shortcut: Ctrl+Shift+Y.

Translate Chapter

Puts English over every bubble. Switch a page back to Japanese at any time.

The first time: reading models

YomiScan reads the page with models that run on your own computer. It needs them once, and asks before downloading anything:

In Chrome

The panel offers to download about 525–550 MB of models (text detection, OCR, the JMdict dictionary and translation) from Hugging Face. They run inside Chrome, on your graphics card when it can.

YomiScan Engine for Windows (optional)

A separate, faster engine that YomiScan starts when needed. Install it from the YomiScan releases page; it downloads its models on first start.

Keep what you learn

Save

☆ Save word and Save sentence in the study card keep them on the Saved page, with search, CSV export and Export for Anki.

Mark known

Known words fold away in the card, and furigana shows only over the words you don't know yet. Import a list you already have from the Saved page.

Your privacy

No account, no analytics, no tracking. Screenshots, page text, saved words and settings stay on this computer. YomiScan connects to the internet only to download its models (once, after you agree) and to fetch the manga pages you ask it to read, from the site that hosts them; Chrome asks before YomiScan may fetch from a new site.
